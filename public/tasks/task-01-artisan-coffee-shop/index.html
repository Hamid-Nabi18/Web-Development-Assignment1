Direct Trade Relationship

Rooted in Sustainable Craft

Founded in 2021, Velvet & Roast partners directly with independent growers across Latin America and East Africa. By paying 150% above fair trade minimums, we support regenerative agriculture and multi-generational farms.

Our beans are roasted weekly in our restored cast-iron drum roaster, calibrated meticulously to unlock the unique aromatic identity of every harvest.
