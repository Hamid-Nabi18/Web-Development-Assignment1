Why Page Scroll Locking Matters

When modal dialogues appear on top of a webpage, maintaining the background scroll position without allowing the underlying page body to scroll is an essential UX requirement. Notice how when the overlay opens, background scrollbars lock instantly and prevent disorienting background movements.

1. Backdrop Dimming

Creates immediate focal hierarchy by obscuring inactive background elements with a dark semi-transparent veil.

2. Multi-Vector Dismissal

Accommodates diverse user preferences by supporting close icon buttons, clicking off the modal, or striking Escape.

3. Accessibility Focus Trapping

Ensures screen readers and keyboard users cannot tab behind the active overlay into obscured viewport areas.
